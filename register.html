Step into the professional fold. Register to secure your active KMRA membership for 2026.

Membership Registration Guidelines

Please review the eligibility criteria and payment requirements before completing the registration form below.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Must be currently employed as a Medical Representative, Area Manager, or Executive in a pharmaceutical or medical diagnostics organization in Sri Lanka.
  • Must be assigned to or operating within the Central Province (Kandy, Matale, or Nuwara Eliya).
  • Must agree to abide by the official KMRA Code of Conduct and Industry Ethics.

Membership Subscription Fees

The registration fee for the year 2026 is LKR 2,000. This fee covers access to all professional seminars, community events, members ID card, and annual networking dinners. Payment details and upload prompts are included directly in the form.
