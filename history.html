Our Heritage

Uncovering the roots, key milestones, and collective journey of KMRA.

Our Mission

To foster professional excellence and ethical standards among medical representatives in Kandy. We strive to provide continuous education, advocacy, and collaborative growth opportunities that elevate the standing of our profession and enhance healthcare outcomes in the community.

Our Vision

To be recognized as the premier professional association for medical representatives in Sri Lanka, championing ethical partnership, digital development, and social responsibility in the pharmaceutical and healthcare sectors.

Historical Journey

Over two decades of building bridges, empowering professionals, and serving Kandy.

2002

The Foundation Circle

KMRA was founded by a passionate circle of 15 medical representatives in Kandy. They aimed to establish a support system to coordinate industry efforts and build a united professional voice in the central province.

2007

Official Incorporation

The association drafted its first constitution and gained official recognition. The first Executive Committee was formally elected, establishing structured sub-committees for ethical standards and professional development.

2012

Decennial Anniversary

KMRA celebrated 10 years of representation. The milestone was marked by hosting the first Kandy Healthcare Symposium and launching the Annual Members Sports Gala to build unity and wellness.

2018

Expanding CSR Footprint

Formally launched the 'KMRA Community Care' initiative. Expanded CSR efforts to provide essential medicines, medical tools, and sanitary infrastructure to underfunded rural clinics in the Kandy district.

2022

20 Years of Excellence

Celebrated KMRA's 20th Anniversary AGM with over 200 active members. Introduced digital education programs and professional accreditation courses in collaboration with healthcare institutes.

2026

A Reimagined Future

Embracing digital systems for members registration, communication, and networking, KMRA enters a new era of enterprise-level connection while holding strong to our core values.
