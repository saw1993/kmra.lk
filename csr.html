CSR Activities

Our hands-on commitment to health, welfare, and social prosperity in the Kandy district.

Community Milestones

Real progress, real impact. Review our latest outreach initiatives.

April 10, 2026 Kandy General Hospital

Annual Health & Blood Donation Camp

KMRA members coordinated a full-day community clinic and blood collection drive. Medical representatives worked alongside government doctors, helping screen and distribute medicines to hundreds of local citizens.

Impact: 150 Blood Units & 300 Patients Treated
January 15, 2026 Alawatugoda Junior School

Rural School Stationery Support Drive

Helping children reach their potential by supplying vital learning items. KMRA members personally funded and distributed school backpacks, notebooks, drawing sets, and shoe vouchers to low-income children.

Impact: 120 Students Fully Supported
KMRA Healthcare support
September 20, 2024 Kandy National Hospital

COVID-19 Ward Support & Supplies

During critical equipment shortages, KMRA responded immediately by procuring and donating essential medical grade PPE kits, sanitizers, and vital medicine stocks to the intensive care wards.

Impact: LKR 500,000+ Essential Supplies Donated
Eco-Conservation Initiatives
November 05, 2025 Hanthana Mountains

Hanthana Green Reforestation Project

Promoting environmental sustainability and protection. KMRA volunteers gathered on the slopes of the Hanthana range to plant native saplings and install banners encouraging tourists to stop littering.

Impact: 500 Native Saplings Planted
