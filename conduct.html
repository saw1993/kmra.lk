Ethical Engagement & Professional Integrity

Medical representatives hold a vital position in the healthcare supply chain, serving as the bridge between pharmaceutical research and clinical practice. KMRA mandates that all members maintain the highest levels of honesty, scientific accuracy, and compliance in their daily interactions with healthcare practitioners (HCPs) and medical institutions.

Core Principles

01

Patient-Centric Integrity

Every scientific presentation, promotional activity, and medicine detailer must put the patient's welfare first. Representatives must ensure that all claims are backed by solid clinical evidence and comply with regulatory bodies.

02

Objective & Scientific Communication

Avoid exaggerated claims, misleading information, or biased reviews. Professional details should be objective, clear, and include safety indicators, warnings, and usage details as validated by scientific publications.

03

Respectful Collaboration with HCPs

Promote professional, respectful, and ethical partnerships with Doctors, Pharmacists, and Nurses. Respect their busy schedules, clinical independence, and hospital policies during clinic calls.

04

Compliance & Professional Boundaries

Adhere strictly to local laws, corporate compliance protocols, and the official Sri Lanka Medical Council (SLMC) guidelines. Avoid any practices that compromise the professional independence of healthcare decision-makers.
